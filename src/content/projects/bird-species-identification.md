An image recognition system to help bird-watchers identify species. Pre-trained VGG-16 and
ResNet18 networks were fine-tuned on the Caltech-UCSD Birds 200 (CUB-200-2011) dataset and
compared. The pipeline covered image pre-processing, data augmentation, training and
evaluation in PyTorch and NumPy.
