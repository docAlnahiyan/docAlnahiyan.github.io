Under review3D MRIThesis
HybridTransMedFormer: a dual-path 3D MRI framework for early Parkinson's detection
A CNN + Transformer model that reads 3D brain scans and flags the disease in its prodromal stage.

Most models need several types of scan to diagnose Parkinson's. This one works from a single 3D T1-weighted MRI by running two paths at once — a convolutional path for fine local detail around the substantia nigra, and a Transformer path for whole-brain context — then fusing them. It reaches diagnosis in the prodromal window, 2–3 years before motor symptoms appear.

92.34% accuracy 0.984 ROC AUC 96.85% prodromal acc.

HybridTransMedFormer

A Dual-Path 3D MRI Fusion Framework for Early Parkinson's Disease Recognition · Khushbu A. N., Zhang X. et al.

Early Parkinson's is hard to catch because the clearest signs appear only after irreversible damage. HybridTransMedFormer reads a single 3D T1-weighted MRI and combines local convolutional features with global self-attention to separate healthy, prodromal, and Parkinson's brains — reaching the prodromal stage 2–3 years before motor symptoms.
